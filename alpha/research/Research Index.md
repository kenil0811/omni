# Research Index

Status: Current reference map

## Rule

Research informs decisions but is not authoritative product or architecture. Current decisions live in the canonical product, architecture, specification, and delivery documents. A report may describe products, prices, APIs, or policies that change; verify time-sensitive claims before implementation or publication. Every retained Word report now carries this status visibly when opened on its own; report-specific recommendations that were considered but not adopted are identified there.

## Current scope and historical evidence

The founder-approved revision keeps a general-purpose App/workflow builder, adds local schedules and public/authenticated browser operation to the first usable release, and makes Windows/native extension boundaries explicit. See `../specifications/Local_Automation_and_Platform_Extension_Profile.md`.

Dated research reports and source archives remain evidence of what was examined, not implementation instructions. References in older reports to post-v0 scheduling/browser or a routine-first substitute reflect the earlier discussion and are superseded by this revision. Preserve objections and rationale; a canonical decision coordinates work but does not prove market demand or technical readiness.
